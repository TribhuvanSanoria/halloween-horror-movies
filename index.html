Scary Games and Movies

My top 3 horror games and top 2 horror movies. Happy Halloween!!

Games

  • Resident Evil 2 Remake

    Zombies in a police station. Mr. X follows you everywhere and its so stressful.

    Scary: 4/5

  • Outlast

    You only have a camera. You cant fight so you just hide in lockers.

    Scary: 5/5

  • Amnesia The Dark Descent

    Dark castle and you go insane if you look at the monster.

    Scary: 4/5

Movies

  • The Exorcist

    A girl gets possessed. Its from 1973 but still creepy.

    Scary: 5/5

  • Hereditary

    A family has a really bad time after a funeral. Dont watch alone.

    Scary: 5/5
